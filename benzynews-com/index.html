Why This Distinction Matters

There is a meaningful difference between:

Breaking-news reporting

and

Evergreen informational publishing.

Breaking news typically depends on:

  • Current events
  • Direct reporting
  • Named sources
  • Rapid updates
  • Chronological developments

An evergreen article, by contrast, may explain:

  • What a product is
  • How a concept works
  • What a company does
  • How a process operates
  • What a particular term means

The reader's expectations should be different for each format.
