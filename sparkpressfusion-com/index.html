Why Domain Verification Matters

Before entering an email address, creating an account, downloading anything, or submitting payment information, verify that you are on the intended domain.

A simple checklist is useful:

  • Check the spelling in the browser address bar.
  • Confirm the website's About and Contact pages.
  • Check whether the branding matches the service you expected.
  • Avoid assuming similarly named domains belong to the same company.
  • Be cautious with unexpected downloads or redirects.
  • Review privacy and terms pages before submitting sensitive information.

This is particularly relevant when several websites use almost identical brand names.
