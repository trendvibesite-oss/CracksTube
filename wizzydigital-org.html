How to Evaluate WizzyDigital Org Before Using Its Services

A website may present an attractive service portfolio, but potential customers should always evaluate the actual scope of work before hiring a digital marketing provider.

Here are some practical checks worth completing.

1. Review the service descriptions

Understand whether the company provides complete campaign management, consulting, website development, or individual marketing services.

2. Ask for relevant examples

Request examples of websites or marketing projects that are similar to your business requirements.

3. Clarify pricing

Ask for a written quotation that separates service fees, advertising expenditure, software costs, and any additional charges.

4. Understand the SEO process

Ask which activities are included, such as technical audits, keyword research, content development, and reporting.

5. Confirm communication arrangements

Find out who will manage your project and how frequently you will receive progress updates.

6. Check ownership and access

Your business should retain appropriate access to its domain, hosting, Google Analytics, Google Search Console, and advertising accounts.

7. Evaluate performance measurement

Understand how the provider measures enquiries, conversions, website traffic, and campaign performance.

The public website describes its service categories, but these practical details should be confirmed directly before entering into a business agreement.
