Guest Post Editorial Guidelines

To maintain high editorial standards for our readers and search engine indexing, all guest post submissions must adhere to the following criteria:

  1. Content Originality: Submissions must be 100% original, unique, and human-written. Plagiarized or auto-generated AI content will be rejected immediately.
  2. Article Length: Articles must be comprehensive and well-structured, containing at least 1000+ words (1200+ words preferred for Tech and Business).
  3. Formatting & Readability: Use clear subheadings (H2, H3), bullet points, and short paragraphs. Avoid dense blocks of text.
  4. Images & Graphics: Include 1 high-resolution featured graphic and 2–3 relevant inline images (or custom diagrams) with proper image alt text.
  5. Outbound Links: You may include 1–2 contextual links to relevant, non-competing websites. No links to spam, adult, casino, or unauthorized download sites.
