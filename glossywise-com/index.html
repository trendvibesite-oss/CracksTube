Common Blogging Mistakes to Avoid

Publishing Without Research

A writer may have a good idea but still publish inaccurate information.

Research before writing.

Writing Only for Search Engines

An article should make sense to a human before it is optimized for search.

Copying Competitors

Taking another article and changing a few words rarely creates meaningful value.

Use competing pages to identify unanswered questions, then create something more useful.

Ignoring Updates

Technology, finance, travel, health, and other fast-changing subjects can become outdated.

Review older articles periodically.

Chasing Word Count

A 1,500-word article that answers the question properly is more useful than a 3,000-word article padded with repetition.
