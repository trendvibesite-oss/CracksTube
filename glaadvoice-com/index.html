How to Evaluate Articles on glaadvoice com

A good-looking website does not automatically make every article authoritative.

Check the Date

Date matters enormously for:

  • Health
  • Market news

Check the Sources

For claims involving statistics, regulations, scientific findings, company performance, or major events, look for supporting sources.

If the information could influence a financial, legal, medical, or business decision, compare it with primary or specialist sources.

Separate Information From Advice

That distinction is especially important for high-stakes subjects.
